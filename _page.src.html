Before you order

Measure your window in two minutes

You don't need anyone to come round. Two numbers and a tape measure is all it takes — and we'll work out the rest.

  1. Measure A — the full length of your curtain rod or track, end to end. Not the glass, the rod. If there's no rod yet, measure the window and add 15 cm each side.
  2. Measure B — from the rod down to where you want the curtain to stop. Just below the sill, or all the way to the floor.
  3. Use centimetres and round to the nearest one. Measure each window separately, even if they look identical.

Questions

Before you ask

How much do curtains cost?

Our standard set — a 3 m curtain with a 3 m sheer — is KSh 4,000, which suits most windows up to about 150 cm wide. Anything wider, or blackout fabric, is quoted on your measurements. Send us your numbers and you'll have an exact price back, not a range.

Do I need you to come and measure?

No. Most of our clients measure their own windows using the guide on this page — two numbers, a tape measure, two minutes. It keeps your cost down and means we can start the same day you message us.

What if I measure wrongly?

Send us a photo of the window along with your numbers. If something looks off against the photo we'll query it before cutting. That catch has saved plenty of orders.

Can I see the fabric before I decide?

Yes — the full collection is on this page under Browse the fabric collection. Each one has a code. Send us the code and we'll tell you what colours that fabric comes in.

What is the difference between a curtain and a sheer?

The curtain is the heavy outer layer that gives privacy at night and blocks light. The sheer is the light inner layer you leave closed during the day — it keeps the room bright but stops people seeing in. Most homes want both, which is why our standard set includes them together.

Do you fit the curtains for me?

Your set arrives finished and ready to hang on an existing rod. Eyelet headings slide straight on, so most clients hang them themselves in a few minutes.

Where are you based, and do you deliver?

We're in Nakuru. Message us on WhatsApp with where you are and we'll confirm delivery to you before you order.
