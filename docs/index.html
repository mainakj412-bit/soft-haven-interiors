Questions

Before you ask

How much do curtains cost?

Our standard set — a 3 m curtain with a 3 m sheer — is KSh 4,000, which suits most windows up to about 150 cm wide. Anything wider, or blackout fabric, is quoted on your measurements.

Do I need you to come and measure?

No. Most clients measure their own windows using the guide above — the rod width and the drop. Send a photo of the window too, and we'll query anything that looks off before cutting.

Can I see the fabric before I decide?

Yes. The full collection is on this page and each fabric has a code. Send us the code and we'll tell you what colours it comes in.

What is the difference between a curtain and a sheer?

The curtain is the heavy outer layer that gives privacy at night and blocks light. The sheer is the light inner layer you leave closed during the day — the room stays bright, but people can't see in.

Where are you based, and do you deliver?

We're in Nakuru. Message us on WhatsApp with where you are and we'll confirm delivery before you order.
